Privacy Policy - "Ryan Shook" (personal Home Assistant / Google Nest app)

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This application is a private, single-household integration operated by Ryan Shook for his own home. It connects his self-hosted Home Assistant instance to his own Google Nest thermostat through Google's Smart Device Management API. It is not offered to, or used by, any other person.

Data accessed

  • Google Nest device data for devices in his own Google Home: thermostat mode and setpoints, ambient temperature, and humidity.
  • Basic Google account identifiers returned by the OAuth sign-in flow, used only to complete authorization.

How the data is used

Data is used only to display and control his own thermostat inside his own Home Assistant instance, which runs on his private home infrastructure and is not exposed to the public internet.

Storage and sharing

  • Data stays on infrastructure under his direct control.
  • No data is sold, rented, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.
  • No other users have accounts or access.

Retention and deletion

Authorization can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings (Third-party access), which immediately ends the app's access. Removing the Nest integration from Home Assistant deletes the stored authorization locally.
